I've played with the idea of writing a love letter (a digital love letter in this case) to you for as long as I could remember.

Just like all the other gestures of romance I thought of in the past or quite recently, this one almost fizzled into thin air until I managed to summon my inner romantic Richard Feynman, because a lengthy reading spree of his love life filled me with thoughts of you.

I adore you Widady, and it makes me feel so warm and fuzzy inside to say this. It is ironic that my brain refuses to churn out new words of affection and adoration for you in a moment where I'm supposedly at my most romantic. Ah shame on me! Feynman the hopeless romantic would be so disappointed!

I guess what I really wanted to tell you is that I adore you. You are the most beautiful woman I know. You are the most amazing woman I know.

Life without you in those 4 years when we weren't talking was so tasteless and painful. Life with you in it (even in the capacity of so called friendship) is extremely warm.

My heart absolutely belongs to you and I love you in ways I will never truly be able to describe.

Always yours,
